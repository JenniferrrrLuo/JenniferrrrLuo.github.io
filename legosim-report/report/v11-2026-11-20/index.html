Recording evidence

126 browsable demonstrations. The collection verifies 113 original movie files and 13 newly rendered files, including alternate views and excerpts where available.

25 episodes rerun in this correction. The signed site listed 864 movie paths, including clips and file aliases.

Limitations

  • 26 of the 140 campaign requests ended without footage. Each outcome links to its input.
  • The attachment profile is experimental. One blinded reviewer preferred default in five of nine study pairs, calibrated in two, and gave two mixed judgments. The signed review belongs to the November 19 study.
