Limitations

  • Historical recovery is in progress. The collection shows the recordings verified at this publication. Queued captures and refused inputs appear with their current outcomes.
  • The correction requested 66 historical JSON episodes. 66 completed the independent motion run and 57 completed native capture.
  • The original archive listed 158 movie paths whose producer could not be confirmed for this edition. The audit records each attribution.
  • 5 of 11 historical Python programs failed during the rerun. Their individual outcomes are in Demonstrations.
