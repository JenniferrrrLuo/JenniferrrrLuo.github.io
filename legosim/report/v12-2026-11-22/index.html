Limitations

  • Human duration distribution agreement is not established; only aggregate real references were available.
  • The 80-run diagnostic uses one exposed three-brick task; it is not an unseen-task or real-human evaluation.
  • No downstream visual policy was evaluated; time-only results measure shortcut availability.
  • Native object-view footage does not certify human skin/contact realism.
  • Authored phases are randomized; native physical trajectories retain their recorded clock.
